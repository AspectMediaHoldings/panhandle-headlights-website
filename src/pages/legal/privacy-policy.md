# Panhandle Headlight Restoration — Privacy Policy

**Publisher:** Panhandle Headlight Restoration is a trade name of Aspect Media Holdings, LLC, a Florida limited liability company. References in this Privacy Policy to "Panhandle Headlight Restoration," "we," "us," or "our" mean Aspect Media Holdings, LLC dba Panhandle Headlight Restoration.

## Who We Are

Panhandle Headlight Restoration operates a mobile headlight restoration service in the Florida Panhandle (Jackson County and surrounding areas). Our website is panhandleheadlights.com.

## Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a quote, or book a service, we may collect:

- Name
- Phone number
- Email address (if provided)
- Vehicle year, make, and model
- Service address (where you want us to come)
- Payment information (processed by Stripe; we do not store card numbers)
- Text message consent and opt-in records
- Photographs of your headlights (before, during, and after service, for our service records and — with your permission — for portfolio use)
- Referral information (if you were referred by another customer, the referring party's name and contact information)

We collect this information when you:

- Submit a contact, quote, referral, or booking form on our website
- Call, text, or email us directly
- Book a service and pay a deposit through our website
- Opt in to receive text messages from us
- Consent (in writing or digitally) during pre-service documentation

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Schedule and confirm your service appointment
- Send you a quote or estimate
- Process your deposit and payment through Stripe
- Send you appointment reminders and service updates by phone, text, or email
- Respond to questions or requests you send us
- Maintain service records for the duration of your guarantee period plus applicable retention
- Handle warranty claims and re-service scheduling
- Track referrals and process referral rewards (10% discount for friend + $10 payout to referrer)
- Comply with legal obligations (tax reporting, 1099 issuance for referral payouts $600+, dispute records)

## Analytics

Our website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first analytics tool. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies, does not track individual users across websites, and does not sell your data. It collects aggregate traffic data such as page views and general visitor location at the country or region level. We use this data only to understand how our website is used. No personal information is shared with Cloudflare through analytics.

See our [Cookie and Analytics Policy](/legal/cookie-policy/) for more detail on cookies and analytics tools.

## Third-Party Service Providers

We share your information only with the following third parties, and only as necessary for the services described in this policy:

- **Stripe** — payment processing. Subject to Stripe's own Privacy Policy at stripe.com/privacy.
- **Netlify** — website hosting and form submission processing. Subject to Netlify's Privacy Policy at netlify.com/privacy.
- **Cloudflare** — website analytics (aggregate only, no personal data). Subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
- **Slack** — internal team notifications when a form is submitted. Subject to Slack's Privacy Policy.
- **Google Workspace** — email delivery for booking confirmations and appointment reminders. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
- **SMS provider** — text message delivery. Subject to the provider's Privacy Policy.
- **Google Business Profile** — public review responses only (does not share private customer information).
- **PayPal or Venmo** — referral reward payouts, only if you are a referrer and elect to receive payment through these channels. Subject to PayPal's or Venmo's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We may disclose your information as required by applicable law or valid legal process, or to protect our legal rights, property, or safety, or that of others.

## Text Messaging (SMS)

If you provide your phone number and consent to receive text messages, we may send you:

- Appointment confirmations and reminders
- Quote follow-ups
- Service completion notices
- Referral reward notifications
- Warranty claim status updates

Text messaging is subject to the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and our consent records. You may opt out at any time by replying **STOP** to any of our messages. To resume receiving messages after opting out, reply **START**. For help, reply **HELP** or contact us at (850) 583-6322.

Message and data rates from your carrier may apply. Message frequency varies depending on your service and appointment status.

## Photographs and Portfolio Use

We photograph headlights before, during, and after service for internal service records and warranty documentation. These photographs are retained with your service record.

We may request separate written consent to use anonymized before/after photographs of your headlights (without any identifying information) for marketing purposes such as our website portfolio, social media, referral cards, and booth displays. Portfolio use is optional and requires your explicit consent. Declining does not affect your service or pricing.

## Data Retention

We retain your information for the following periods:

- **Service records** (name, vehicle information, service date, coating applied, guarantee period, before/after photos): duration of your guarantee period plus two (2) years
- **Payment records**: as required by applicable law (typically seven years for tax purposes)
- **SMS consent records**: minimum of four (4) years per TCPA best practices
- **Booking form consent audit trail** (per-document acceptance records): seven (7) years to align with Florida statute of limitations for contract disputes
- **Voicemail call logs and text message records**: seven (7) years to support dispute resolution
- **Referral tracking records**: as long as the referrer remains an active customer, plus two years
- **Marketing consent records** (portfolio photo use): until you revoke consent or ten (10) years, whichever is longer

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your personal information we hold
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete information
- **Request deletion** of your information, subject to legal retention requirements (we cannot delete records we are legally required to keep, such as payment records for tax purposes)
- **Opt out** of text messaging by replying STOP
- **Withdraw consent** for portfolio photo use at any time (photos will be removed from active marketing use within 30 days; photos already printed on physical collateral will not be reprinted specifically to remove your images)
- **Request a copy** of information we have about you in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at (850) 583-6322 or [info@panhandleheadlights.com](mailto:info@panhandleheadlights.com). We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.

## Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Measures include:

- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) on our website
- Access controls limiting who can view customer records
- Stripe's PCI-compliant payment processing (we do not store card numbers)
- Regular backups of customer records
- Retention limits per the schedule above

No system is 100% secure. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable Florida law.

## Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, contact us and we will delete it.

## Florida-Specific Privacy Considerations

We comply with Florida consumer privacy protections including the Florida Deceptive and Unfair Trade Practices Act (FDUTPA) and applicable Florida Statutes governing business record retention and consumer disclosures.
