We are a mobile service. We come to your location. You are responsible for providing a safe, shaded area for us to work and ensuring the vehicle is accessible at the scheduled time.

### Add-On Charges (retail only)

A single add-on charge, starting at $20 and quoted on-site before work begins, may apply to a retail job in any of the following situations:

- **Heavy oxidation:** lenses that require additional wet-sand time to remove severe yellowing or deep degradation.
- **Larger headlights:** trucks, vans, SUVs, or vehicles with larger-than-standard light housings.
- **Extensive damage or scratches:** additional repair time required to address surface damage before coating.

Only one add-on charge applies per vehicle regardless of the number of applicable factors. The customer is quoted the total add-on price before work begins. Add-on charges do not apply to fleet pricing (fleet is flat per vehicle regardless of condition or size).

Prices are subject to change with notice under the Changes to These Terms section below. Your quoted price at the time of booking, including any confirmed add-on, will be honored for your scheduled appointment.

## 5. Pre-Service Documentation and Pre-Existing Lens Conditions

Headlight lenses may have pre-existing conditions that are not visible before restoration begins. These include but are not limited to: internal moisture or water damage, internal oxidation, delamination of the factory UV coating, micro-cracking or crazing within the polycarbonate, spider-cracking or stress fractures, heat discoloration, deep scratches from prior physical contact, damage from previous restoration attempts, seal or housing failure, UV degradation below the lens surface, and paint or chemical overspray.

If we identify any such pre-existing condition before or during work, we will:

1. Stop before proceeding with restoration.
2. Show you the condition and document it with a photograph.
3. Obtain your written or digital signature acknowledging the pre-existing condition and consenting to proceed at your own risk regarding that condition.

Work performed after your written or photo-documented acknowledgment of a pre-existing compromised lens condition is not covered by our [Service Guarantee](/legal/service-guarantee/) with respect to the acknowledged condition. You will receive a copy of the pre-service documentation with your service receipt.

If you decline to consent to proceed after a pre-existing condition is identified, no work is performed on the affected lens, and no fee is charged for that lens.

We are not responsible for damage, appearance issues, or lens failure caused by conditions that existed before our service began and were disclosed to you.
