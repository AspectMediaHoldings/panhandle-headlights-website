# Panhandle Headlight Restoration — Cookie and Analytics Policy

**Publisher:** Panhandle Headlight Restoration is a trade name of Aspect Media Holdings, LLC, a Florida limited liability company. References in this policy to "Panhandle Headlight Restoration," "we," "us," or "our" mean Aspect Media Holdings, LLC dba Panhandle Headlight Restoration.

## Overview

This policy explains how panhandleheadlights.com handles cookies and website analytics. We have chosen tools specifically to minimize data collection and protect visitor privacy.

## Cookies

Our website does not use advertising cookies, behavioral tracking cookies, or third-party cookies that follow you across other websites.

We do not use:

- Google Analytics
- Facebook Pixel or Meta tracking
- Advertising or retargeting cookies
- Session recording or heatmap tools

## Cloudflare Web Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns on our website. Cloudflare Web Analytics is a privacy-first tool with the following characteristics:

- Does not place cookies on your browser
- Does not track individual users across websites or sessions
- Does not build individual user profiles
- Does not sell or share personal data
- Collects only aggregate information: page views, general visitor geography (country or region level), browser type, and device type

No personal information is collected or shared with Cloudflare through the analytics tool. We use this aggregate data only to understand how our website is performing and how visitors find and use our pages.

For more information about Cloudflare's privacy practices, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

## Stripe Payment Processing

When you click the deposit payment link on our website, you are directed to a Stripe-hosted payment page at checkout.stripe.com or a Stripe payment link. Stripe may use cookies and browser storage technologies on their own payment pages to enable payment processing, fraud prevention, and their own analytics. Stripe's use of your data is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy, not this policy.

We do not control Stripe's data practices. We do not receive or store your payment card information. Stripe transmits payment confirmation to us after your transaction is complete.

## Netlify Forms (Booking and Referral Forms)

Our booking and referral forms are handled by Netlify Forms, our website hosting platform. Netlify Forms uses server-side processing to capture form submissions and does not require cookies for form functionality. Form data (name, phone, email, vehicle information, referral information) is transmitted to our internal notification systems (email + Slack) and stored in Netlify's form submission database.

Netlify's use of form submission data is governed by Netlify's Privacy Policy, available at netlify.com/privacy. We do not use Netlify Forms for behavioral tracking or advertising purposes.

## Do Not Track

Because we do not use behavioral tracking or advertising cookies, you do not need to configure special browser settings to avoid being tracked on our website. We do not track individual visitors.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, our website's behavior does not change — we already do not track you.

## Cookies You May Encounter on Third-Party Pages

When you leave our website via a link to a third-party site (including Stripe's payment page, our Google Business Profile, or any social media link), the third party's cookie and tracking practices apply. We do not control third-party cookies.
