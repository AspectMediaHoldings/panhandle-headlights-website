## 3. Pre-Service Documentation

Before beginning work, our technician will inspect each lens for pre-existing conditions that may limit the achievable result or affect [Service Guarantee](/legal/service-guarantee/) coverage. Such conditions include, but are not limited to:

- Cracking, chipping, or structural fractures in the lens material
- Delamination of the interior factory coating
- Internal moisture, fogging, or condensation between lens layers
- Deep pitting or damage below the outer surface
- Signs of prior improper restoration or coating failure
- Heat discoloration from engine or exhaust exposure
- Paint or chemical overspray on the lens

If we identify any such pre-existing condition, we will:

1. Stop before proceeding with restoration.
2. Show you the condition and document it with a photograph.
3. Obtain your written or digital signature acknowledging the pre-existing condition and consenting to proceed at your own risk regarding that condition.

Work performed after your written or photo-documented acknowledgment is not covered by our Service Guarantee with respect to the acknowledged condition. You will receive a copy of the pre-service documentation with your service receipt.

If you decline to consent to proceed after a pre-existing condition is identified, no work is performed on the affected lens, and no fee is charged for that lens.

Panhandle Headlights will photograph the lenses before and after the work. Restoration may make an existing crack or internal problem more visible once the outer haze is removed. That is the pre-existing condition showing through, not damage caused by Panhandle Headlights.

## 4. What Customer Understands and Accepts

Customer acknowledges and accepts the following:

1. Restoration is a cosmetic and functional improvement, not a guarantee that the lens will look brand new. Results vary with the age, material, and prior damage of the lens.
2. The process involves sanding close to painted panels and applying a coating. Panhandle Headlights masks and protects the surrounding area, but Customer accepts that working around the lens carries normal risk, which Panhandle Headlights takes reasonable care to avoid.
3. Deep damage, internal fogging, moisture, or a failed inner coating cannot be corrected by outer restoration and are not the responsibility of Panhandle Headlights.
4. On heavily degraded lenses, restoration may extend usable life but may not fully remove all discoloration.
5. Post-service care instructions must be followed: no vehicle wash for 24 hours after coating application; no wax, polish, or protectant directly on the restored lens for 7 days.
6. The add-on charge, if any, is quoted on-site before work begins based on the technician's assessment of extra time required for heavy oxidation, larger headlights, or damage repair. Customer approves the quoted add-on charge before Panhandle Headlights proceeds with the service.
