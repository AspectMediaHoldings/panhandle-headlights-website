## Results Vary

Headlight restoration results depend on the condition of your lenses before service. Lenses with surface oxidation and normal weathering typically achieve excellent clarity after restoration. Lenses with internal damage, deep scratches, delamination, or advanced UV degradation may not fully restore to a new appearance. We will assess your lenses before beginning work and provide an honest evaluation of expected results.

We do not guarantee any specific cosmetic outcome. We do guarantee the coating we apply for the period stated in our [Service Guarantee](/legal/service-guarantee/), under normal conditions and proper post-service care.

## Pre-Existing Lens Conditions

We are not responsible for damage or conditions that existed before our service began. The restoration process (wet sanding through multiple grits) may make pre-existing conditions more visible by removing surface oxidation that was masking them. This is not damage caused by our work. Pre-existing conditions that may affect results or become more visible during restoration include:

- Internal moisture, condensation, or water intrusion into the lens housing
- Internal oxidation (yellowing or hazing beneath the lens surface)
- Delamination of the factory UV coating
- Micro-cracking or crazing within the polycarbonate substrate
- Spider-cracking or stress fractures in the lens
- Heat discoloration from exhaust, engine heat, or sun exposure
- Deep scratches or scuffs from prior physical contact
- Damage from prior restoration attempts using incorrect products or techniques
- Seal or housing failure allowing moisture entry
- UV degradation extending below the surface layer
- Paint, tar, or chemical overspray on the lens
- Road chip damage or impact cracking
- Any other structural or subsurface defect not caused by our work

When we identify pre-existing damage before or during service, we stop work, show you the condition, and document it with a photograph. We obtain your written or digital signature acknowledging the pre-existing condition before proceeding. This process is described in detail in our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service/) and [Service Guarantee](/legal/service-guarantee/).

## Florida UV Environment

The Florida Panhandle receives UV Index levels of 10 to 11 for much of the year — among the highest sustained UV exposure in the continental United States. Polycarbonate headlight lenses in this environment degrade faster than manufacturer general estimates, which are typically based on national or northern climate averages.

Our guarantee periods are specifically calibrated for Florida UV conditions. We select coatings whose documented performance supports the guarantee periods we offer in this environment. That said, no coating is permanent, and reapplication will be needed when the guarantee period ends.
