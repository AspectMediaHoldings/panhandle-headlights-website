# Panhandle Headlight Restoration — Service Guarantee

**Publisher:** Panhandle Headlight Restoration is a trade name of Aspect Media Holdings, LLC, a Florida limited liability company. References in this Service Guarantee to "Panhandle Headlight Restoration," "we," "us," or "our" mean Aspect Media Holdings, LLC dba Panhandle Headlight Restoration.

## What We Guarantee

Panhandle Headlight Restoration guarantees the workmanship on the restored outer lens surface for the period specified by your service tier, measured from the date of service.

| Tier | Coating | Guarantee Period |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Clear Coat | 3M professional clear coat | 6 months |
| Clear Coat Pro | Delta Kits Infinity 4.1 water-based coating | 18 months |
| Clear Coat UV | GlasWeld UV-cured urethane hard coat | 36 months |

All tiers include wet sanding and polishing. If your headlights develop visible hazing on the treated outer surface within the guarantee period under normal use and care, we will re-service them at no charge.

## Pre-Service Documentation

Before beginning work, our technician will inspect each lens for pre-existing conditions that may limit the achievable result or affect guarantee coverage. Such conditions include, but are not limited to:

- Cracking, chipping, or structural fractures in the lens material
- Delamination of the interior factory coating
- Internal moisture, fogging, or condensation between lens layers
- Deep pitting or damage below the outer surface
- Signs of prior improper restoration or coating failure

If we identify any such pre-existing condition, we will:

1. Stop before proceeding with restoration.
2. Show you the condition and document it with a photograph.
3. Obtain your written or digital signature acknowledging the pre-existing condition and consenting to proceed at your own risk regarding that condition.

Work performed after your written or photo-documented acknowledgment of a pre-existing compromised lens condition is not covered by this guarantee with respect to the acknowledged condition. You will receive a copy of the pre-service documentation with your service receipt.

If you decline to consent to proceed after a pre-existing condition is identified, no work is performed on the affected lens, and no fee is charged for that lens.

## What the Guarantee Does Not Cover

The guarantee does not apply to the following:

- **Physical damage.** Rock chips, impacts, abrasion, scratches, or cracks that occur after service are not covered.
- **Internal fogging or condensation.** Moisture between lens layers is a seal failure inside the headlight assembly. It cannot be corrected by exterior restoration and is not covered by this guarantee.
- **Pre-existing lens damage acknowledged before service.** Conditions documented and acknowledged in accordance with the Pre-Service Documentation section above are excluded from coverage.
- **Harsh chemical exposure.** Application of the following products directly to the restored lens surface voids the guarantee: acidic wheel cleaners, alkaline (high-pH) degreasers, paint strippers, solvents (acetone, methyl ethyl ketone, gasoline), tire dressing containing silicone or petroleum distillates, or other products designed to strip coatings or paint. This exclusion does not apply to standard hand washing or drive-through car washes using pH-neutral soap.
- **Failure to follow post-service care instructions.** Do not wash the vehicle for 24 hours after service. Do not apply wax, polish, or protectant directly to the restored lens for 7 days after service.
- **Body, paint, or collision repair performed after service.** Overspray, masking tape adhesive, chemical exposure, or abrasion from subsequent vehicle work voids the guarantee on affected lenses.
- **Lenses not serviced by Panhandle Headlight Restoration.** The guarantee applies only to lenses we personally restored.

## Coverage Determinations and Customer Evidence

If we determine that a claim is not covered — for example, due to chemical exposure or post-service damage — we will provide our reasoning to you in writing (email or text). You may submit reliable evidence supporting continued coverage, including product labels, receipts, car wash records, dash camera footage, or witness statements. We will consider your evidence in good faith before finalizing the coverage determination.

Coverage determinations regarding chemical exposure are based on visible coating damage patterns consistent with chemical attack, professional judgment, and any evidence provided by both parties.

## Florida UV Environment

The Florida Panhandle has a UV index of 10-11 year-round — approximately twice the northern U.S. average. Our guarantee periods are set with this environment in mind and represent realistic expectations for each coating chemistry under these conditions. We do not extrapolate durability ratings from cooler climates.
