# Panhandle Headlight Restoration — Cancellation and Deposit Policy

**Publisher:** Panhandle Headlight Restoration is a trade name of Aspect Media Holdings, LLC, a Florida limited liability company. References in this policy to "Panhandle Headlight Restoration," "we," "us," or "our" mean Aspect Media Holdings, LLC dba Panhandle Headlight Restoration.

## Booking Deposit

A $25 deposit is required to hold your appointment. The deposit is applied in full toward your total service cost at job completion — it is not an additional fee. Deposits are collected securely through Stripe.

## Cancellation by the Customer

### More than 24 hours before your appointment

Cancel or reschedule by calling or texting (850) 583-6322. Your $25 deposit will be held as a credit and applied to your rescheduled appointment. Deposit credits are valid for 90 days from the original appointment date. After 90 days, forfeited credits are not reinstated.

### Less than 24 hours before your appointment (late cancellation)

The $25 deposit is forfeited. You are welcome to rebook, and a new deposit will be required to hold the new appointment.

### No-show

If you are not present or reachable at your scheduled service address at the agreed time and we receive no prior notice, the deposit is forfeited. A new deposit is required to rebook.

### Unsafe or Unsuitable Site Conditions

If a technician arrives at the scheduled service address and cannot safely perform work due to conditions the customer failed to disclose or provide — including but not limited to: no shaded work area, no vehicle access, unsafe surroundings (active construction, hazardous chemicals, aggressive pets), the vehicle being unavailable, or refusal to permit service — this is treated as a late cancellation. The $25 deposit is forfeited. A new deposit is required to rebook.

Site condition requirements are stated in your service confirmation and in our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service/). If you are uncertain whether your site meets these requirements, contact us at (850) 583-6322 before your appointment.

## Cancellation by Panhandle Headlight Restoration

We may occasionally need to cancel due to equipment issues, illness, or conditions that prevent safe work. If we cancel your appointment:

- We will notify you by phone or text as early as possible.
- Your deposit will carry forward to the rescheduled date, or be refunded in full — your choice.
- Refund processing time depends on your card issuer's schedule and is not within our control. See "Refund Processing Times" below.

## Weather

All restoration work is performed in shade. We do not require clear skies, but we cannot work in active rain or lightning. If weather prevents service on your scheduled day, we will contact you to reschedule. Your deposit carries forward at no penalty, or you may request a full refund — your choice.

## Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform obligations under this policy due to causes beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to: acts of God, natural disasters, severe weather events, government orders, power outages, disease outbreak, civil disturbance, or infrastructure failure.

In such cases, we will contact you to reschedule at no penalty. Your deposit carries forward to the rescheduled date, or may be refunded in full at your request.

If rescheduling within 60 days of the original appointment date is not possible, we will **automatically refund your deposit in full** within 5 to 10 business days after the 60-day rescheduling window expires. No customer action is required. We will notify you by phone, text, or email when the refund has been initiated and provide the Stripe transaction reference for your records.

## How to Cancel or Reschedule

Call or text (850) 583-6322. Text cancellations are accepted and timestamped from the moment we receive them. Voicemail cancellations are timestamped based on the time the message is received and logged by our voicemail system. In the event of a dispute regarding transmission timing, we will consider reliable evidence submitted by both parties, including carrier records, phone bills, or other delivery confirmations. For time-sensitive cancellations, text is faster and provides the clearest audit trail.

## Refunds on Completed Work

We do not offer cash refunds on completed services. If you are dissatisfied with the result, contact us within your guarantee period. Valid concerns are handled through re-service under our [Service Guarantee](/legal/service-guarantee/).

**Delivery of Service Guarantee:** We provide the Service Guarantee document to you at the time of booking, delivered via email, text, or through your booking confirmation portal. Please review it before your appointment. If you did not receive it, contact us at (850) 583-6322.

**Precedence:** In the event of any conflict between this Cancellation and Deposit Policy and our Service Guarantee, the Service Guarantee governs matters specifically addressing guarantee coverage, exclusions, and re-service remedies. Cancellation, deposit, no-show, site conditions, weather, force majeure, and refund matters are governed by this Cancellation and Deposit Policy.

## Refund Processing Times

We use commercially reasonable efforts to initiate refunds through Stripe within three to five (3-5) business days of the refund decision. The time required for the refund to then appear on your card statement depends on your card issuer's or bank's processing schedule, which is not within our control. Typical bank processing times range from 3-10 additional business days after we initiate the refund. Some banks may take longer.

If your refund has not appeared after 15 business days from our initiation, contact us at (850) 583-6322 and we will provide the Stripe transaction reference so you may follow up with your card issuer directly.
